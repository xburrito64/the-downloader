When a download finishes

Four ideas, each played on a small copy of the window. All of them are short (under a second), drawn in the same 3-pixel steps as the forest, cost nothing while nothing finishes, and become a plain instant change when motion is set to Reduced. They can also be combined.

1. Spore puff

The mushroom in the title bar hops and puffs out a little cloud of pink spores. The row glows once. Works on every screen, because the title bar is always visible.

2. Firefly to the Library

A firefly lifts off the finished song and flies to the Library tab, which lights up with a +1. It shows where the file went.

3. A mushroom grows

Each finished download grows a small mushroom on the forest floor at the bottom of the window. Over a long playlist the floor fills up. They are gone the next time the app starts. Press it a few times.

4. Carved stamp

A small wooden "Got it" plank thumps onto the row, with a few pink sparks. The original stamp idea, redone in the forest style.
