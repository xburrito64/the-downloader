Nunito, for everything you read

Titles, artists, settings and messages. Rounded and warm, so the pixels stay an accent.

Motion

  • Things answer when touched. Cards lean toward the pointer, buttons sink, the slot gulps a link.
  • One arrival. Cards are dealt in once when a link is read. Nothing else enters with a flourish.
  • A finished download gets stamped, with a short burst of sparks, once.
  • Nothing moves on its own. No pulsing, floating or flicker while idle. The scanlines are a still overlay.
  • Reduce motion turns all of it into plain instant changes.

Yours to decide

  • Palette: Forest or Emerald are the two greens; Plum, Lagoon and Daybreak are still here to compare. More than one can ship as themes.
  • Your art: the wisp in the title bar is a placeholder. The mascot, the stamp and empty-screen pictures are natural spots for your own drawings.
  • Title bar: this drawn one, or the normal Windows one. The drawn one fits the look; the Windows one behaves exactly as you expect.
  • How loud: more tilt and bounce, or less.
